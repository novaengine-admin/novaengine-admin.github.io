## Notes

Region names in your data must match the names below **exactly**, including capitalisation and any suffix.
{: .warning }

- The map also contains non-geographic entries (`Migratory - Offshore - Shipping (NT)`, `No usual address (NT)`, `Unincorporated NT`). Ignore them unless your data uses them.
