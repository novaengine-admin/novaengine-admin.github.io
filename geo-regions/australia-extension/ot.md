## Notes

Region names in your data must match the names below **exactly**, including capitalisation and any suffix.
{: .warning }

- `Christmas Island` is included as a region of this map.
- The map also contains non-geographic entries (`Migratory - Offshore - Shipping (OT)`, `No usual address (OT)`, `Unincorp. Other Territories`). Ignore them unless your data uses them.

---

## Region names

4 regions.

- Christmas Island
- Migratory - Offshore - Shipping (OT)
- No usual address (OT)
- Unincorp. Other Territories
