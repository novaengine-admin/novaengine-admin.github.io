## Notes

Region names in your data must match the names below **exactly**, including capitalisation and any suffix.
{: .warning }

- The map also contains non-geographic entries (`Migratory - Offshore - Shipping (ACT)`, `No usual address (ACT)`, `Unincorporated ACT`). Ignore them unless your data uses them.

---

## Region names

3 regions.

- Migratory - Offshore - Shipping (ACT)
- No usual address (ACT)
- Unincorporated ACT
