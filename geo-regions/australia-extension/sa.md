## Notes

Region names in your data must match the names below **exactly**, including capitalisation and any suffix.
{: .warning }

- Where an LGA name is shared with another state, the name carries a suffix: `Campbelltown (SA)`, `Kingston (SA)`. Use the full name including the suffix.
- The map also contains non-geographic entries (`Migratory - Offshore - Shipping (SA)`, `No usual address (SA)`, `Unincorporated SA`). Ignore them unless your data uses them.
