## Notes

Region names in your data must match the names below **exactly**, including capitalisation and any suffix.
{: .warning }

- `Cocos (Keeling) Islands` is included as a region of this map.
- The map also contains non-geographic entries (`Migratory - Offshore - Shipping (WA)`, `No usual address (WA)`). Ignore them unless your data uses them.
